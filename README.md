Grow an Incrementally Growing Garden

You're too lazy to grow a garden though, so you bought a robot to do it for you! It's not great for now, but incrementally it will get better!

Get a robot to garden for you, because AI is better, except it isn't for now. Buy upgrades and stuff to make your garden prosper!

Your robot has the ability to bend reality... Use it to your advantage!
